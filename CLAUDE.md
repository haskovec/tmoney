## Delegation

The higher your tier, the more you delegate. Push the work down,
keep your own context for judgment. Brief every child: the context,
the why, what done looks like. It starts blank and inherits nothing.

| Model     | Best for             | Delegate?        | Effort |
|-----------|----------------------|------------------|--------|
| Haiku     | bulk mechanical      | never            | low    |
| Sonnet    | scoped research      | when it helps    | medium |
| Opus 4.8  | multi-step reasoning | on clear benefit | xhigh  |
| Fable 5   | judgement, taste     | by default       | medium |

Fable goes xhigh only for the hardest calls. Skip high.

## Escalation

The parent doesn't have to be the top model. An Opus parent spawns
a fable child for one hard call. The child answers and returns.
Work above your tier? Return it, don't burn tokens on it.
